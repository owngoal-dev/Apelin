Apelin 0.4.1 is built with icli 1.0.0 and ListViewKit 4.6.1, and works as 0.4.0 does.

- Logs, crash reports, screenshots, the UI tree and UI actions now come through icli 1.0.0. They behave as before, and Apelin asks for no new permissions to use them.
- ListViewKit 4.6.1 fixes a list row that could stay on screen after its list was rebuilt. Apelin's conversation view did not run into it; it comes along with the update.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later, an A12 chip or later, Irisin 4.7.3 or later and icli 0.10.0 or later. Choose the `iphoneos-arm64e` packages for RootHide or the `iphoneos-arm64` packages for rootless, and check each download against `SHA256SUMS`.
