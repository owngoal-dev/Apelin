Apelin 0.4.6 shows a chat at work the moment you send, says when it is waiting for the network, and tells you what went wrong in one line.

- After you sent a message, nothing showed under it until the chat had been opened in the background, which with no network or a slow provider could take a long time. "Thinking…" now shows under your message at once, and Stop is there from the same moment: it puts a message that has not gone out yet back in the message field.
- With no network, a chat read "Reconnecting…" for minutes before it failed. It now reads "Waiting for Network…" as soon as the first request fails, and carries on by itself when the network is back.
- An error in a chat was the provider's own text, drawn like a reply. It is now a red line that names the problem (No Internet Connection, Unable to Reach Model Provider, Request Timed Out, Sign-In Not Accepted, Usage Limit Reached, Too Many Requests, Model Provider Error or Chat Too Long), with what to do or what the provider said under it, in your language.
- A message that could not be sent because the device was offline or the provider was out of reach brought up an alert with a technical message, sometimes only after half a minute. The message now goes back in the message field with a short "Message Not Sent" notice, and a sign-in that cannot be renewed is given up on after 20 seconds.
- A reply from an OpenAI model whose connection broke halfway failed the turn. It is now asked for again.
- Antigravity lists Gemini 4 Hydrogen, and the built-in model lists are refreshed.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later, an A12 chip or later, Irisin 4.7.3 or later and icli 0.10.0 or later. Choose the `iphoneos-arm64e` packages for RootHide or the `iphoneos-arm64` packages for rootless, and check each download against `SHA256SUMS`.
