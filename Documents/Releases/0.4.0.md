Apelin 0.4.0 lets you continue a conversation after a model sends a tool call with no name.

- A conversation could fail on every new message with an error such as "Invalid 'input[33].name': empty string", sometimes only after you switched model or provider, and trying again did not help. A model had sent a tool call with no name, and it stayed in the conversation's history. Apelin now leaves that call and its result out of what it sends to the model, and the conversation still shows them as before.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later, an A12 chip or later, Irisin 4.7.3 or later and icli 0.10.0 or later. Choose the `iphoneos-arm64e` packages for RootHide or the `iphoneos-arm64` packages for rootless, and check each download against `SHA256SUMS`.
