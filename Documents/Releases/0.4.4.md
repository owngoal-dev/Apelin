Apelin 0.4.4 adds Trae, Trae CN and Raccoon to Model Providers, reads WorkBuddy's own model list, and puts the model row above the message field.

- Trae and Trae CN (ByteDance's IDE, its international and China builds) and Raccoon (SenseTime's office assistant) could not be used as model providers. Each now signs in in the browser under Settings ▸ Model Providers, and Fetch Models lists the account's own models. Thanks to JanXiao, who wrote all three and tested them with real accounts.
- WorkBuddy and WorkBuddy AI offered a fixed list of models that missed what an account's plan adds. Fetch Models now reads the account's own list, with the reasoning levels each model takes.
- A model request that failed on a weak connection gave up after about fifteen seconds, and a provider that stayed silent for a minute timed out. Requests are now tried again for about a minute and a half, and a slow provider is waited on for up to five minutes.
- The model, reasoning and speed controls sat under the message field. They are now above it, so the model a chat runs with is the first thing you read.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later, an A12 chip or later, Irisin 4.7.3 or later and icli 0.10.0 or later. Choose the `iphoneos-arm64e` packages for RootHide or the `iphoneos-arm64` packages for rootless, and check each download against `SHA256SUMS`.
