Apelin 0.3.6 brings back your projects on rootless, keeps conversations open and usable after a restart, says up front that roothide Bootstrap cannot build, and lets tweaks hook Apelin's own classes.

- On rootless, the projects you made were missing from the project list, their links said the project could not be found, and their conversations were removed each time Apelin opened. Apelin was reading a different folder from the one your projects are in. It now reads the same folder and removes a project's conversations only after it sees that project's folder go. The first time 0.3.6 opens, it moves your chats and other files from the old folder into the right one. Conversations removed before this update cannot be brought back by Apelin.
- When a conversation was interrupted while the agent waited for your approval, such as by a restart, it kept an approval card that Approve and Deny could not clear, so no message could be sent until you started a new chat. The card now goes away, and you can send again.
- After the device restarted, Apelin opened on a new chat instead of the conversation you were in, which looked as if the conversation was gone. It now opens the conversation you were in.
- On roothide Bootstrap, setting up a toolchain failed its self-test with "Bad executable" and the toolchain was removed again. Bootstrap runs only programs it signs itself and does not sign Apelin's build tools, so Apelin cannot build there. It now says so on the welcome page and before setting up or importing a toolchain, instead of downloading one that cannot run.
- A tweak could not hook most of Apelin's own methods. Apelin's classes now expose their methods and properties to the Objective-C runtime, so a tweak can hook them with MSHookMessageEx.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later, an A12 chip or later, Irisin 4.7.3 or later and icli 0.10.0 or later. Choose the `iphoneos-arm64e` packages for RootHide or the `iphoneos-arm64` packages for rootless, and check each download against `SHA256SUMS`.
