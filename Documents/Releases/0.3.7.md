Apelin 0.3.7 adds Antigravity as a model provider.

- Antigravity was missing from Model Providers, so its models could not be used in a conversation. It is now there: sign in with your Google account in Safari, then pick the models you want, such as Gemini 4 Argon or Claude Opus 5.5.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later, an A12 chip or later, Irisin 4.7.3 or later and icli 0.10.0 or later. Choose the `iphoneos-arm64e` packages for RootHide or the `iphoneos-arm64` packages for rootless, and check each download against `SHA256SUMS`.
