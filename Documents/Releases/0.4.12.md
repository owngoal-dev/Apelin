Apelin 0.4.12 updates kwwk to 0.1.139, removes the Antigravity provider, and refreshes the built-in model catalog.

- After updating to 0.4.11, signing in to Antigravity in Settings ▸ Model Providers succeeded and listed models, but sending a chat failed asking for an AirRelay address that individual users cannot obtain or configure. Upstream kwwk 0.1.139 removes the relay-only Antigravity provider, so Apelin 0.4.12 removes Antigravity from Model Providers, refreshes the built-in model catalog, and displays kwwk `0.1.139` in the Model Providers footer. Any saved Antigravity sign-in and enabled models from 0.4.11 or earlier versions are removed on upgrade, and a chat that had an Antigravity model selected switches to another signed-in account's model or asks you to choose one. To use Gemini models directly with your own key, sign in to Google AI Studio or OpenRouter in Settings ▸ Model Providers.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later, an A12 chip or later, Irisin 4.7.3 or later and icli 0.11.0 or later. Choose the `iphoneos-arm64e` packages for RootHide or the `iphoneos-arm64` packages for rootless, and check each download against `SHA256SUMS`.
