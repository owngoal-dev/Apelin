Apelin 0.3.5 makes the Chinese text read more naturally.

- In Simplified and Traditional Chinese, some wording read awkwardly or differed from the words iOS uses for the same thing. The translations are now smoother and use the same words as the system.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later, an A12 chip or later, Irisin 4.7.3 or later and icli 0.10.0 or later. Choose the `iphoneos-arm64e` packages for RootHide or the `iphoneos-arm64` packages for rootless, and check each download against `SHA256SUMS`.
