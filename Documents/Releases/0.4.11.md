Apelin 0.4.11 updates kwwk to 0.1.138, restoring OpenCode Go and custom `opencode.ai` chats, updating Antigravity sign-in and models for AirRelay, and refreshing the built-in model catalog.

- Chats to OpenCode Go through a custom endpoint, or to Anthropic `/v1` and OpenAI Responses endpoints on `opencode.ai`, could fail with missing session header errors, doubled `/v1/v1/messages` paths, or WebSocket 404 rejections. Apelin updates kwwk to 0.1.138 so every `opencode.ai` request sends the required OpenCode session header, Anthropic `/v1` addresses are not doubled, and OpenAI Responses falls back to HTTP streaming when WebSocket is rejected.
- Direct Antigravity Code Assist access has been retired and Google returned account verification 403 errors; signing in could also collide with another process using local port `51121`, and starting a chat without an AirRelay endpoint configured reported that no sign-in was saved. Settings ▸ Model Providers now signs in to Antigravity through Google on a dynamic local port, offers the updated Antigravity model list with reasoning effort built into each model choice, preserves a configured AirRelay address when you sign in again, and explains clearly when an AirRelay endpoint is needed.
- Newer models from OpenCode, OpenRouter, Z.AI, Hugging Face, Together, Fireworks, and other providers were missing from the built-in catalog, some chats on Anthropic and relay providers could lose thinking blocks or fail compaction with an empty summary, and Settings ▸ Model Providers showed the previous kwwk version in its footer. Apelin refreshes the built-in model catalog from kwwk 0.1.138, improves thinking replay and compaction reliability across providers, and displays kwwk `0.1.138` in the Model Providers footer.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later, an A12 chip or later, Irisin 4.7.3 or later and icli 0.11.0 or later. Choose the `iphoneos-arm64e` packages for RootHide or the `iphoneos-arm64` packages for rootless, and check each download against `SHA256SUMS`.
