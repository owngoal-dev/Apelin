Apelin 0.4.2 lets you sign in to Claude from another device when its page will not open on yours, and keeps an installation Irisin lost track of.

- Claude Pro or Max could not be signed in to on iOS 16: the sign-in page said "This page can't be shown because your browser or device software is out of date", and there was no other way in. Every account sign-in page now shows its sign-in link, and a tap copies it. Open the link in a browser on another device and sign in there. For Claude, that browser ends on a page it cannot open: copy that page's address, then tap Paste Redirect Address in Apelin to finish. A sign-in that shows a code can be finished the same way, by entering the code on the other device.
- Installing a package you built could end with "Irisin could not complete the installation" although the package was installed. Apelin then listed an older installation and could not remove the new one. When Irisin does not report a success, Apelin now checks whether the package was installed, and keeps the installation if it was.
- Claude Fable 5.2, Kimi K3.1 Pro and Gemini 4 Carbon are in the model lists.

Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.0 or later, an A12 chip or later, Irisin 4.7.3 or later and icli 0.10.0 or later. Choose the `iphoneos-arm64e` packages for RootHide or the `iphoneos-arm64` packages for rootless, and check each download against `SHA256SUMS`.
